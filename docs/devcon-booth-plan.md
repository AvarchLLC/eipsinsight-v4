# EIPsInsight @ Devcon — Booth Ideas

Quick reference for the Devcon (India, 2026) booth.

**Two things to get right:**
1. **Use Glamsterdam, not Fusaka.** Fusaka already shipped (Dec 3, 2025). Glamsterdam is the *next* fork — live on Sepolia now, mainnet ahead. That's the countdown/readiness story. (ePBS + Block-Level Access Lists are the headliners.)
2. **Educational only.** Devcon India bans sales/solicitation/trading. We're an observability tool, so we're fine — just frame everything as *"see & understand Ethereum,"* never buy/trade/invest. No paid-tier pitching, no QR to exchanges. MEV stuff = "how it works," never "how to profit."

---

## Things to DO at the booth

### On the big screen (runs all day, pulls people in)
- **Glamsterdam countdown + readiness** — what's shipping next, client-by-client, live. Starter for every Ethereum person walking by.
- **Live ACD decision ticker** — recent core-dev decisions scrolling like a stock ticker. Hook: *"this is what the devs agreed last week — most people don't know it's public."*
- **Dependency galaxy** — our AA/PQ dependency graphs projected big as "the map of Ethereum standards." Most photogenic thing we have.
- **EIP of the hour** — rotating spotlight auto-pulling a famous EIP's live story.

### Hands-on (tablets — people compete, friends gather to watch)
- **"Type your favorite EIP"** — swoop-search at the front edge. *"Type any number, we'll show you its story."* Best icebreaker.
- **EIP trivia** — 3 quick questions, get it right → sticker. Trivia is the #1 crowd magnet at dev booths.
- **"Which persona are you?"** — 3 taps → drops them into the right dashboard (dev / editor / researcher / builder / newcomer).
- **"What are the devs deciding?"** — hand them the decision ledger, let them scroll real ACD calls.
- **"Editor for a minute"** — show a real EIP PR + the editorial checklist. People are stunned it's this transparent.
- **Trace a proposal's journey** — pick an EIP, watch it animate draft → final with the calls where it was debated.

### Signature sit-down (the one thing people remember)
- **"Build your EIP watchlist in 60 seconds"** — set their persona, add the proposals they care about. They leave with a personalized tool, not a flyer.

### Scheduled moments (create crowds at fixed times)
- **Mini office-hours** — 15-min walkthroughs ("how to read a fork," "how ACD decides"). Put the schedule on the backdrop.
- **"Ask an editor"** slot — if we can get an EIP editor to sit for an hour.

### The wall (engagement + free user research)
- **"Which EIP do you care about most?"** — people write on it all day. Photo op + research we can post on X.

## Decoration & physical stuff

### Backdrop / structure
- Big backdrop: logo + **one-line pitch** + QR + office-hours schedule.
- The **dependency galaxy** on the big screen doubles as the visual centerpiece / photo wall.
- **"Map of Ethereum Upgrades" timeline poster** — floor-to-ceiling, doubles as backdrop and photo spot.
- **"How Ethereum decides" poster** — the editors + how a proposal becomes Final.

### Table + swag (the logo walks out the door)
- **Stickers with the URL** — laptop stickers are Devcon currency; our logo rides laptops all over the hall.
- **EIP trading cards** — collectible set for landmark EIPs (1559, 4337, 7702, 4844…), stats on the back. A *set* → repeat visits.
- **"State of Ethereum Standards" one-card** — printed handout with live numbers (total EIPs, # Final this year, next fork date). Print a fresh batch each morning.
- Whiteboard / big paper + markers for the wall.

### Gear
- 2 tablets (kiosk/full-screen, auto-reset) + 1 big screen/TV + stands.
- Power + backup battery + **offline hotspot** (conference wifi will die — cache key dashboards so demos survive).

---

## Capture (keep it educational)
- **QR everywhere** → a simple "try it" page on eipsinsight.com.
- **The ask after a demo:** *"Leave your email/Telegram, we'll send you the fork-readiness tracker when Glamsterdam ships."* Give a reason (future value), not a mailing-list pitch.
- **Adopt-an-EIP watch** — leave contact to get pinged when one EIP they care about changes status. Feels like a gift.

---

**Spine:** galaxy on the big screen pulls the crowd → greeter hands off to the 60-second watchlist → close at the sticker/EIP-card table with a QR opt-in. Trivia + the wall run all day.
